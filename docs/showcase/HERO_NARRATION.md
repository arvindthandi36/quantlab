# Hero narration draft · Arvind Thandi

Target: 60–90 seconds, about 170 words. Speak naturally and leave short pauses for the transitions.

> QuantLab is an event-driven quantitative finance laboratory. I built it to understand how
> quantitative models translate into actual trading and risk decisions.
>
> On the trading desk, I can submit a market or limit order and follow it through a price-time-priority
> order book. These fills come from the matching engine, and flow into cash, positions and P&L.
>
> The synthetic market has a hidden value process and participants with different information.
> That value influences their orders; it does not directly set the transaction price.
>
> In the options lab, I can inspect Greeks and execute a stock hedge. Risk then lets me compare
> VaR, Expected Shortfall and stress scenarios. Stat Arb connects regression to residuals,
> signals and execution, including cases where the relationship breaks.
>
> The research tools keep uncertainty, selection bias and untouched evaluation visible.
> Explain mode connects a result to its inputs, mathematics and limitations.
>
> This is a learning and research simulator, not production trading infrastructure or evidence
> of real-world alpha. The point is to make the mechanisms inspectable—and the assumptions
> difficult to ignore.
