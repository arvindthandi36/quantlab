Implied volatility & root finding

Find the volatility that reproduces a price. A low pricing error does not always identify volatility reliably.

Newton divides the price error by vega. If that derivative is too small or the step leaves a safe bracket, the solver falls back to bisection.

Monte Carlo vs analytical pricing

IID terminal paths use risk-neutral drift, consistent with the pricing assumptions.

Sampling error typically shrinks with 1/√n. For these vanilla European contracts, the analytical formula is faster; simulation is a learning and validation tool.
