What just happened

    Estimated relationship

    Y ≈ α + βX. The fit uses past observations. The residual is Y − α − βX; it differs from Y − X when scales differ.

    Quant Mode · OLS, units and numerical method

    Minimise Σ(Yᵢ − α − βXᵢ)². With design A of shape n × 2 and response y of shape n × 1, θ̂ = (AᵀA)⁻¹Aᵀy has shape 2 × 1 when full rank. QuantLab computes centered/scaled least squares using SVD; it never explicitly inverts AᵀA.

    β is X units per Y unit for this unit hedge. Dollar neutrality instead requires qX·X + qY·Y = 0. Neutrality to the observed X-return proxy requires qX·X + qY·Y·estimated loading = 0. Neither proves neutrality to an unobserved market factor.

    Residual diagnostics

    Descriptive AR(1), not ADF. No p-values or pass/fail cointegration claims. A fitted half-life is a conditional characteristic, not a deadline for convergence.
